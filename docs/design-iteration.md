# 界面迭代：让信息和操作融入球场

[English](#english)

## 场景

竖屏比赛需要同时容纳球队、教练、队徽、比分、手牌和操作按钮。添加说明或按钮很容易挤压卡面；而如果每个功能使用不同材质，也会破坏整个比赛画面的统一感。

## 迭代方向

项目的视觉以较暗的球场、油画式人物和克制的暖色文字为基础。界面调整优先考虑球员卡是否仍然可辨、主要操作是否容易触及、教练与队徽是否与阵容保持协调，然后再处理材质和过渡。

例如，对局内的教练战术和手牌重抽入口，设计经过多次调整：从显眼但突兀的亮边矩形，逐步收敛到更贴合原界面的暗色轮廓，同时保留可点击区域。分享阵容的画面也从固定模板调整为围绕真实阵容和成绩组织信息，避免用固定人物代替玩家的球队。

## 怎么检查

分别查看常见和较小的移动视口，检查按钮点击区域、卡片比例和长文本；测试比赛选择、计分及弹窗等不同状态。截图展示具体画面，交互测试用于检查功能；两者都不能代替目标设备上的实际体验。

# Interface iteration: making information feel at home on the court

## The context

A portrait match screen must accommodate the lineup, coach, badge, score, hand, and controls. New explanations or buttons can easily compress the cards; inconsistent materials can also make individual controls feel detached from the court.

## The direction

The visual language uses a dark court, painterly player portraits, and restrained warm text. Interface changes first consider whether cards remain readable, whether the primary actions are reachable, and whether the coach and badge remain in harmony with the lineup. Material and motion follow those priorities.

For example, the coach tactic and hand-redraw controls went through several revisions: bright outlined rectangles gave way to a darker silhouette closer to the existing interface, while retaining the touch area. Lineup sharing was also reworked around the actual lineup and results rather than substituting a fixed character for a player’s team.

## Evaluation

Review common and smaller mobile viewports for touch targets, card proportions, and long text. Check different states, including hand selection, scoring, and dialogs. Screenshots communicate the visual result, while interaction tests check behavior; neither alone replaces the experience on the target device.
